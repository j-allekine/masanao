# Default municipal LGU Offices

## Decision

Use a small, municipality-specific starter catalog. It should contain the
offices corresponding to officials that the Local Government Code says must
exist in **each municipality**. Add locally created offices only when the
municipality actually maintains them.

This is a catalog recommendation, not an organization chart or staffing rule.
An Office is the operational owner of an activity or supply request; it does
not assert that a particular position is filled.

## Recommended default catalog

The labels below translate the statutory municipal officials into Masanao's
usable starter names. The Mayor's Office uses the requested `MO` abbreviation;
the other rows use the `Municipal` prefix and no abbreviation.

| Default Office | Abbreviation | Statutory basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mayor's Office | MO | Municipal mayor |
| Municipal Vice Mayor |  | Municipal vice-mayor |
| Municipal Sangguniang Bayan |  | Sangguniang Bayan members |
| Municipal Sangguniang Bayan Secretariat |  | Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan |
| Municipal Treasurer |  | Municipal treasurer |
| Municipal Assessor |  | Municipal assessor |
| Municipal Accounting |  | Municipal accountant |
| Municipal Budget |  | Municipal budget officer |
| Municipal Planning and Development |  | Municipal planning and development coordinator |
| Municipal Engineering / Building Official |  | Municipal engineer/building official |
| Municipal Health |  | Municipal health officer |
| Municipal Civil Registrar |  | Municipal civil registrar |
| Municipal Cooperatives Development |  | Municipal cooperatives development officer |

Section 443(a) uses "there shall be in each municipality" for this set. The
Cooperatives Development Officer was added as a mandatory municipal official by
RA 11535 in 2021; the post may be full-fledged or merged with a related office,
unit, or department. Do not create a generic "Municipal Hall" row: it is a
location, not an operating unit.

## Do not add as universal defaults

Section 443(b) says the mayor **may appoint** the following officials. They
are common and may be appropriate for a specific municipality, but they are not
safe universal defaults:

- Municipal Administrator
- Municipal Legal Officer
- Municipal Agriculturist
- Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Officer
- Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer
- Municipal Architect
- Municipal Information Officer

The Sangguniang Bayan may also retain other existing offices, create offices
needed for municipal purposes, or consolidate functions for efficiency and
economy. Therefore, do not presume locally named units such as a Tourism,
Disaster Risk Reduction and Management, Public Employment Service, General
Services, or Supply Office exist everywhere. Create them after confirming the
municipality's local ordinance or current organization.

## Supply-operations note

For Masanao, the **Municipal Treasurer** is the safer municipal
default for supply/procurement accountability. The Code assigns municipal
treasurers, not a municipal general services officer, to fill municipal supply
orders and call bids. A municipal General Services Office may be a valid local
office, but its addition should be confirmed locally rather than inferred from
the provincial/city rule.
